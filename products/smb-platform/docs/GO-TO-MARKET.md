# Go-To-Market Playbook: Local Boost

## Launch Strategy: The GBP Audit Trojan Horse

The GBP Audit Tool (Product 1) IS the GTM engine. It's not a separate product — it's the top of the funnel.

### Funnel Flow

```
Free GBP Audit (web form)
  ↓ Email capture
PDF Report with score + competitor comparison
  ↓ Shows what's broken
CTA: "Fix this for $99/mo" or "Let us manage it for $149/mo"
  ↓ Trial signup
14-day free trial of Local Boost
  ↓ Automated onboarding
Paying customer ($99-149/mo)
  ↓ Results → referrals
Organic growth
```

### Week 1: Seed the Market (Manual)

**Goal: 10 free audits delivered, 2-3 paying customers**

1. Generate audit reports for 50 local businesses in Canton/Woodstock using the GBP Audit Tool
2. Print the PDF reports (or email them)
3. Walk into 10 businesses, hand them the report:
   - "I noticed your Google profile is missing [X]. Here's a free report showing what's wrong and how to fix it."
   - If they're interested: "I can fix all of this for $99/month. You'll get more calls within 30 days."
4. For businesses you can't visit, email the report with a personal note

**Target categories (easiest sell):**
- HVAC contractors (high-value leads, understand marketing ROI)
- Dental practices (competitive on reviews, care about appearance)
- Auto repair shops (high review sensitivity)
- Restaurants (review-dependent, high search volume)

### Week 2-4: Digital Lead Gen

**Channel 1: Facebook Groups (Free)**
- Join Cherokee County community groups, Woodstock/Canton groups
- Post: "I'm offering free Google Business Profile audits for local businesses this week. DM me your business name and I'll send you a report."
- Expect 20-50 responses per post in active groups
- Convert 10-15% to paid = 2-7 customers per post

**Channel 2: Google Business Profile (Ironic, Yes)**
- Create a GBP listing for the consulting/tool business
- Optimize it perfectly (eat your own cooking)
- Target searches: "marketing help Canton GA", "Google business help near me"

**Channel 3: Chamber of Commerce**
- Cherokee County Chamber: $250-400/year membership
- Sponsor or present at a lunch-and-learn: "Why Your Business is Invisible on Google"
- Attendees are warm leads

**Channel 4: Cold Email with Proof**
- Generate audit reports for 200 local businesses
- Email: "Your competitor [name] has 3x more reviews and ranks higher on Google. Here's a free report showing why. I can fix this."
- Expected response rate: 5-10% → 10-20 conversations → 3-5 customers

**Channel 5: Referral Program**
- One month free for every referral that converts
- Local businesses talk to each other → plumber refers electrician refers HVAC
- Referral is the #1 growth channel for local SaaS

### Month 2-3: Content + Automation

**LinkedIn content strategy (for consulting flywheel):**
- 3x/week posts about local business digital presence
- Case studies: "How we improved [business]'s Google visibility 40% in 30 days"
- Cross-sell: consulting leads discover the tool, tool leads discover consulting

**Local SEO content:**
- Blog posts: "Best [category] in Canton GA" pages
- Rank for local search terms
- Each page features (and links to) businesses using Local Boost

**Automated email nurture:**
- Audit leads who don't convert get weekly emails:
  - Week 1: "3 things killing your Google visibility"
  - Week 2: "Why reviews matter more than you think"
  - Week 3: "Your competitor just got 5 new reviews this month"
  - Week 4: "Limited offer: first month free"

## Pricing Strategy

### Anchoring
- Show competitors first: "Podium: $399/mo. Birdeye: $299/mo."
- Then: "Local Boost: $99/mo. Everything they offer, at a price that makes sense."

### Free Tier (Lead Gen Only)
- Free GBP audit report (one-time)
- Requires email to download PDF
- No ongoing access

### Trial
- 14-day free trial
- Full Growth plan features
- Credit card required (reduces tire-kickers)
- Auto-converts to Starter at day 14

### Annual Discount
- 20% off for annual commitment
- Reduces churn (annual customers churn 50% less than monthly)
- Cash flow benefit: $948 upfront vs $99/mo

## Sales Playbook

### The 5-Minute Pitch

> "Let me show you something. I ran your business through our Google audit tool. [Show PDF report] You scored a 42 out of 100. Your competitor [name] scored a 78. That means when someone searches for [your service] in [your city], they're more likely to find [competitor] than you.
>
> The good news: most of this is fixable. The three biggest things: [cite top 3 recommendations from report].
>
> I built a tool that fixes all of this automatically. It optimizes your Google profile, sends review requests to your customers after every job, and writes professional responses to your reviews using AI. You don't have to do anything — it runs in the background.
>
> It's $99 a month. Most businesses see a 30-50% increase in Google calls within the first 60 days. Want to try it free for 14 days?"

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't need more customers" | "That's great — but are you getting the RIGHT customers? We also help you get more reviews, which increases trust and lets you charge premium prices." |
| "I already do my own marketing" | "That's why I brought this report — it shows what's working and what isn't. This tool handles the Google side so you can focus on what you're already doing." |
| "$99/month is too expensive" | "I understand. Let me ask: how much is one new customer worth to you? If you're a plumber and an average job is $300, this pays for itself with one extra call per month." |
| "I'll think about it" | "No problem. Keep the report — it's yours. When you're ready, the 14-day free trial is always available. Here's my card." |
| "I tried something like this before" | "What was the issue? [Listen] The difference with us is simplicity — you don't log into anything. We handle everything and text you a monthly summary." |

## Metrics to Track

### Leading Indicators
- Free audits generated per week
- Audit-to-trial conversion rate
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate
- Customer GBP score improvement (proves value)

### Lagging Indicators
- MRR and ARR
- Monthly churn rate
- Net revenue retention
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target > 3:1)

### Operational Metrics
- SMS delivery rate
- Review request conversion rate
- AI response approval rate
- Time to first review (after signup)
- Support tickets per customer
